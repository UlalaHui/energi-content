# Video Recipe (extracted from 3 Energi Elite reels)

Source reels: battery heat, V2L "power bank on wheels", 12V battery. Full transcripts in `reels/transcripts.md`.

## Format
- Length: 60-80s; script 160-190 words
- One topic, one surprising angle: "EV facts nobody tells you"
- About 15-20 scenes, 3-4s each, smooth camera moves and morphs, few hard cuts

## Structure
| Beat | Time | What happens | Example from their reels |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0-3s | Surprising claim or question tied to Malaysian life, 12 words or fewer, with a visual metaphor | "If your EV bakes under the Malaysian sun every day, is the battery slowly dying?" |
| Myth check / setup | 3-12s | State the worry, then a number that reframes it | "Battery cells handle up to 60 degrees. Malaysian weather is 35." |
| Explain | 12-45s | 2-3 beats, each one number or analogy | Laptop vs fridge cooling; 60 kWh vs 15-20 kWh a day; tiny 12V vs big battery |
| Twist / real risk | 45-60s | "Here's the part nobody tells you" | Heat on top of heat; 12V goes flat while main battery is 80% |
| Practical takeaway | 60-70s | One action for the viewer | Ask what cooling system it uses; check if your car has V2L |
| CTA | last 3-5s | Fixed line | "Follow for more EV facts nobody tells you." |

Optional: a series cliffhanger before the CTA ("That's exactly what we're showing in the next video.")

## Writing rules
- Second person ("your EV", "your porch")
- At least 2 Malaysian references (weather, porch, tarmac, nasi kandar, TNB, terrace house, balik kampung)
- Round, memorable numbers; analogies to everyday objects (power bank, laptop, fridge)
- Short sentences; spoken rhythm; no jargon without a one-line explanation (e.g. "BMS, a battery management system")
- Product is not pitched in the body; Energi Elite appears via the logo and end card

## Hook patterns to rotate (for the 10 variants)
1. Question about a daily habit ("If your EV...")
2. Shock number ("Your car holds 800 power banks.")
3. Hidden danger ("The battery that can strand you isn't the big one.")
4. Myth bust ("Heat isn't what kills your battery.")
5. Local scene ("Your porch at midnight is...")
6. Warning ("Never park your EV this long.")
7. Comparison ("Your EV vs your house: ...")
8. "Nobody tells you" opener
9. Cost angle ("For the price of a plate of nasi kandar...")
10. Direct challenge ("Check this before you buy an EV.")

## Captions on screen
- Word groups (1-4 words) in the lower third, synced to the voiceover
- Preset A: white bold, no box (heat and 12V reels)
- Preset B: uppercase white with an orange highlight box on the key word (V2L reel)
- Optional bold text hook at the top in the first 2s (12V reel: "NEVER PARK YOUR EV THIS LONG")

## Branding
- Semi-transparent ENERGI ELITE logo, top centre, the whole video
- End card: black background, Energi Elite logo, 2-3s
