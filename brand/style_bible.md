# Visual Style Bible (for every generated scene)

Extracted from keyframes of the 3 reels. Add this block to every Higgsfield image/video prompt, plus 1-2 reference frames from `brand/ref-frames/`.

## Base prompt suffix
> Clean 3D product-render style, soft studio lighting, smooth blue-grey gradient background, glossy surfaces, gentle reflections on the floor, cinematic shallow depth of field, high detail, no text, no logos, no brand badges, no emblems on the car.

## Recurring elements
| Element | Description |
|---|---|
| Hero car | Modern compact electric SUV, metallic **burnt-orange** paint, unbadged, clean front grille, LED light bar |
| Studio | Seamless blue-grey studio, soft top light, subtle floor reflection |
| Battery | Flat EV battery pack shown as a cutaway under the car or floating: grey casing, rows of cylindrical cells, glowing blue (cool) or red/orange (hot) |
| Energy | Glowing blue/cyan lines for power flow and cooling; orange-red glow for heat |
| Home | Malaysian two-storey terrace house with car porch, warm interior lights; night scenes in deep blue |
| Explainers | Floating 3D objects for comparisons (thermometer, power bank, small houses), clean and minimal |
| People | Stylised 3D characters (Pixar-like) for human moments; rare |

## Colour palette
- Brand orange (car, highlights, caption box): #E4632E (measured from their logo)
- Background blue-grey: #8A9BB0 to #C9D3DE gradient
- Cool accent: cyan/blue glow
- Heat accent: orange-red glow
- Night: deep navy

## Camera
- Slow push-ins, orbits around the car or battery, smooth morph transitions
- Vertical composition in their originals; for 1:1 keep subjects centred

## Never
- Wall-mounted chargers or the E1 (use real photos instead)
- Any real car brand logo, especially Tesla
- Text baked into generated images (all text is added in post)
- Photorealistic people faces that could be mistaken for real staff
